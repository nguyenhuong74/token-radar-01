What would the token be worth?

Every protocol below records no token yet. Each is priced by a model fitted to the tokens that already trade — a log-log regression of market cap on TVL — and the fit was chosen by a blind test, not by its author: five pricers were each fitted on four fifths of the traded set and priced the fifth, and the winner is the one the board uses. Ranks carry the range they took over 200 bootstrap refits, and every farm plan is netted to the dollar: an expected claim, its costs, and a verdict of FARM, THIN or SKIP. The board does not predict whether or when a token issues — it prices the record as it stands.

How the value is priced — and the test it passed

implied value = fitted base × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Fitted base — — The five pricers: the log-log ones regress log10(market cap) on log10(TVL), the offset one adding each category's mean residual shrunk toward zero with a pseudo-count of 3 and clamped to half a decade, and the ratio pricers use median market-cap/TVL. No name is special-cased: every coefficient is refitted from the snapshot on every run.
  • Growth — 1 + 0.6 × 7-day TVL change, clamped −50% to +100%.
  • Maturity — 0.70 + 0.30 × age up to 3 years; a protocol that ran longer with no token prices higher.
  • Breadth — +3% per extra chain, up to +12%.

The blind test

Five pricers, each fitted on four fifths of the — traded tokens and priced on the fifth it had never seen, over 5 folds. Median error is the middle held-out token's price error as a times factor; the winner is the lowest. The board is priced by the winner alone.

Rank ranges

The winning fit was refitted 200 times on bootstrap resamples of the traded set and the board re-ranked each time. Every row's range is the 10th–90th percentile of the ranks it took: a rank of 12 with a range of 9–18 is a steadier claim than a rank of 12 with a range of 4–60.

Farm economics — the assumptions, in the open

Expected claim = implied value × 10% farmer allocation × (deposit ÷ eligible farmer deposits, taken as 5% of TVL). Costs: $20 of gas plus the deposit's opportunity cost at 5% a year over the hold. FARM clears $25 net, THIN clears $0, SKIP does not cover its costs. The fitted model pays small protocols more per dollar of TVL, so the best net farms sit low on the board — sort by Net to find them.
