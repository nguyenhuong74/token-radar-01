What would the token be worth?

Every protocol below records no token yet. Each is priced from the market itself: the median market-cap/TVL ratio of live-token protocols in its own category, adjusted for seven-day growth, age and chain breadth. The board does not predict whether or when a token issues — it prices the record as it stands, and shows how to farm each protocol before and after a launch.

How the value is priced

implied value = TVL × category comp × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Category comp — the category's median mcap/TVL, shrunk toward the market median — with a pseudo-count of 3, so a thin category leans on the market and a deep one stands on its own; clamped 0.05–1.50.
  • Growth — 1 + 0.6 × 7-day TVL change, clamped −50% to +100%.
  • Maturity — 0.70 + 0.30 × age up to 3 years; a protocol that ran longer with no token prices higher.
  • Breadth — +3% per extra chain, up to +12%.
